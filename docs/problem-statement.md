# Problem Statement

## My Story

Recently, I unexpectedly got shortlisted for an **Amazon SDE Internship interview** with only **10 days left to prepare**.

At first, I thought preparation simply meant revising DSA and solving problems.

But instead of studying, I found myself spending hours researching:

- Reading interview experiences on **Leetcode Discuss, GeeksforGeeks, Medium, and Reddit**
- Understanding what Amazon actually asks
- Figuring out which topics matter most
- Deciding how much focus to give to behavioural preparation
- Trying to create a realistic day-by-day study plan

The biggest problem was that every source said something different, and I ended up spending more time researching than actually preparing.

That’s when I realised something:

Every candidate preparing for the same company repeats almost the exact same process — manually researching scattered information, extracting patterns, prioritising topics, and planning under pressure.

It felt repetitive.

And repetitive problems are often good candidates for automation.

That idea became the motivation behind this project.


## Target Users

This workflow is designed for:

- Students preparing for **SDE internships**
- Final-year students targeting **SDE-1 / New Grad**
- Early-career software engineers with **limited preparation time (5–15 days)**


## The Pain Point

Candidates spend hours on **research before preparation**:

- Reading scattered interview experiences
- Understanding company-specific patterns
- Prioritising topics
- Planning what to study each day

This process is repetitive, stressful, and often inefficient.


## Why This Matters

Interview preparation time is short and fixed.

Every hour spent researching is an hour not spent on:

- Solving problems
- Revision
- Behavioural preparation
- Mock interviews

Poor planning directly reduces interview readiness.


## Proposed Solution

This project automates interview research and planning using an **Agentic n8n Workflow**.

The workflow:

1. Researches interview experiences  
2. Extracts repeated company-specific patterns  
3. Diagnoses readiness  
4. Prioritises high-ROI topics  
5. Creates a realistic preparation roadmap  
6. Generates behavioural preparation

> Spend less time researching and more time preparing.


## What the Workflow Produces

A personalised prep report generated in ~1 minute:

1. Interview Intelligence Report  
2. Reality Check  
3. Personalized Strategy  
4. High ROI Topics  
5. Curated Problem Set  
6. Day-by-Day Roadmap  
7. Behavioural Prep Pack  
8. Motivation

> The output is intentionally **short and actionable**, optimized for **free API tiers**.
