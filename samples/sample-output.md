# Interview Prep Plan — Amazon (SDE-1 / New Grad)

**10 days left · 4h/day · SPRINT mode**

> Illustrative sample of the assembled report. Exact wording varies per run and per model

## 1. Interview Intelligence Report
Amazon SDE loop: online assessment, then 3–4 rounds mixing DSA + Leadership Principle
behavioural questions. Coding is medium difficulty, heavy on practical problem solving.

**Commonly tested topics**
- Arrays & hashing
- Trees / BFS-DFS
- Graphs
- Dynamic programming
- Two pointers / sliding window

## 2. Reality Check (no sugar-coating)
> You "finished DSA once" — that's a vibe, not a skill. 10 days is enough to be dangerous if
> you stop re-watching tutorials and start solving timed problems today. Your weak trio (DP,
> graphs, tries) overlaps Amazon's favourites. Fix that first.

## 3. Why this plan exists
Sprint mode front-loads your high-frequency weak topics (graphs, DP) while keeping your strong
areas warm, so even partial completion maximises expected score.

## 4. High-ROI topics (do these first)
- Graphs (BFS/DFS, topological sort)
- Dynamic programming (1-D, then 2-D)
- Trees (traversals, LCA)
- Heaps / intervals

**Safe to skip / deprioritise:**
- Advanced tries
- Bit manipulation tricks

## 7. Behavioural / HR Prep
*Use STAR; lead with impact and quantify the Result. Map every story to a Leadership Principle.*

**Values this company screens for**
- Customer Obsession
- Ownership
- Bias for Action
- Dive Deep

**Practice questions**
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate.
- Describe a deadline you nearly missed and what you did.
- A time you took ownership beyond your role.

**STAR story skeleton**
S: team project slipping → T: I owned the integration → A: cut scope, paired daily, added tests
→ R: shipped 2 days early, reused by 2 other teams.

## 8. Confidence Booster
You have 10 days and a plan. Most candidates walk in with neither. Solve out loud, ask
clarifying questions, and trust the process.
